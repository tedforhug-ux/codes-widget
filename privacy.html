Codes widget — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

What data the extension accesses

With your permission, Codes widget uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. It lists recent messages and reads the sender, subject and body of messages from senders you configured in the extension's rules, in order to find one-time security codes.

How the data is used

  • Messages are processed only inside your browser to extract a security code and show it to you.
  • Extracted codes are kept in temporary browser storage for about 10 minutes and are then deleted.
  • Message content is not saved, except a short text snippet of a matching message in which no code was found, kept for the same 10 minutes so you can view it.
  • OAuth tokens and your settings are stored locally in the browser's extension storage.

What the extension does not do

  • It has no server. Your data is not sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising, credit decisions or to train AI models.
  • It does not send, modify or delete email.

Limited Use

Codes widget's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect a mailbox in the extension settings, remove the extension, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing the extension deletes all of its local data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: github.com/tedforhug-ux/codes-widget/issues.

Кратко по-русски

Расширение читает письма Gmail только на чтение, только от отправителей из ваших правил и только в вашем браузере, чтобы найти код подтверждения. Коды хранятся около 10 минут и удаляются. Сервера нет, данные никому не передаются, письма не отправляются и не изменяются. Отключить доступ можно в настройках расширения или на myaccount.google.com/permissions.
